# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public visitors are potential buyers browsing a specific realtor's public catalog URL. Realtors are authenticated catalog owners who manage their own developer and project content. The platform owner manages realtor clients and subscription limits through a separate local owner app.

## Product Purpose

The product is a multi-realtor developer catalog platform. It helps each realtor publish a curated public catalog of developers and projects at their own URL, while potential clients can browse without an account. Success means each realtor can manage only their own content, subscription limits are enforced, and public visitors can move from realtor catalog to developer pages to project details with clear orientation and realtor contact actions.

## Brand Personality

Direct, structured, grounded.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a broad contractor marketplace, a search-heavy listing portal, or a generic soft real estate landing page. Avoid public Google Drive/document buttons, ornate card grids, decorative gradients, and contact flows that push buyers away from the realtor by default.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the Template B system: flat surfaces, strong green/neutral palette, firm borders, compact navigation, and clear selected states.
- Keep the sidebar as a fixed orientation tool, not content that scrolls away.
- Make cards behave like obvious clickable surfaces instead of relying on extra CTA buttons.
- Show the current realtor's contact details as the default public contact source.
- Scope every catalog surface to the current realtor.
- Keep realtor profile data separate from subscription and entitlement data.
- Keep motion brief and purposeful: load and scroll reveal should clarify structure, not delay the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic links for clickable cards, reduced-motion support, and responsive navigation that works on mobile without relying on hover.
